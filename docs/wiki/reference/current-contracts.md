## Owner functions

**Current source:** `officialFootballRosters.preview/apply` reconciles reviewed official football observations for 2005–2026 with exact identity mappings, a source fingerprint, an expected data revision, and a current backup manifest. Optional annual `listedClass` and `rosterOnly` separate roster facts from eligibility and participation. Public and owner season selectors reach 2005; the bounded global player catalog now includes up to 2,000 people. See [the import guide](../guides/official-football-rosters.md) for retained fields, protections, and hosted verification status.

`rosterAdmin.login` exchanges `CFB26_ADMIN_KEY` for a random token whose SHA-256 hash and expiry are stored server-side. `sessionStatus` and `logout` validate/revoke it. The secret must be at least 24 characters and never enters `VITE_*` configuration.

The owner contract includes:

- create Person, enroll/decommit prospect, start/close stints, and overwrite Player Seasons;
- add repeatable evaluations, draft outcomes, nflverse identities, and weekly NFL gap records;
- dry-run/apply roster and Player Game imports, with blocking-error abort, explicit preview acknowledgement, and a current-revision backup before apply;
- preview/apply season rollover;
- export paginated Michigan datasets and create a version-3 fingerprint/manifest bound to the Michigan data revision;
- preview affected records, then merge duplicate people or delete an erroneous person transactionally;
- set season rules and actual conference champions;
- resolve provider identities without heuristic matching; and
- edit identity/background, roster facts, measurements, eligibility evidence/override, honors, source URLs, and phase GP/snaps in the scrollable roster spreadsheet; add enrolled players or committed recruits as rows and save up to 200 changed rows in one revision-checked transaction through `rosterSpreadsheet.save`;
- view source, identity, operation, revision, audit, backup, eligibility, and publication health.

Every consequential Michigan owner mutation advances `michiganDataRevisions` and inserts an `ownerAuditEvents` record with actor/session, action, target, timestamps, result, warnings, and backup reference where applicable. Import, rollover, merge, and delete reject a manifest whose revision no longer matches. `migrations.retireUnversionedBackupManifests` audits and deletes only old server-side manifests; downloaded files remain unchanged.

`seasonalStats.upsertPlayerGame` enforces the zero/null/grade invariant and Michigan roster/game identity. A person/game pair is unique.

**Current source:** `rosterAdmin.previewParticipationImport` and `applyParticipationImport` support reviewed cumulative screenshot totals on Player Seasons. Both require an owner session; apply also requires the preview revision and a current backup manifest. Imports match roster identities, reject stale/decreasing totals, preserve other phases and overall games played, and record source fingerprints. Player profiles display the phase totals separately from game-derived grades. See [screenshot participation imports](../guides/screenshot-participation-imports.md).

## Rating and ranking contract

- Power is a neutral-field points scale with explicit prior weight, version, calibration, units, home field, and sample state.
- Power ranks the complete season-specific FBS schedule field from `1` through the field size; it has no fixed top-25 or top-50 publication cutoff, so historical and future counts can differ.
- When no weekly edition or season composite exists, the complete-field fallback uses current-season Elo where present, then the prior-season composite, then prior-season Elo, and finally a visible neutral baseline. It never leaves a scheduled FBS team unranked silently.
- Ranking tables share compact schedule-style rows and a content-sized maximum width. They lead with rank, team, and the named rating. Power exposes its record plus offense, defense, and available special-teams components. Program omits record, shows compact cutoff-safe national- and conference-title marks from the latest 25 seasons with age-based fading, stacks conference marks two high, and uses retained conference identity for league-specific art. Its four evidence columns expose raw 0–100 results, acquisition, development, and honors scores; the overall rating weights them 50/20/10/20. Résumé alone exposes Q1–Q4 records alongside its weighted results, performance, history, and season-strength evidence. Model diagnostics and edition basis remain on Methodology rather than in every row.
- Résumé appears from Week 7 and uses the approved 70% results plus 30% capped-performance split, followed by the bounded own-history bonus.
- Edition snapshots drive historical quadrants, schedule strength, and playoff/ballot evidence; future data is never substituted.
- Scheduled 2026-forward prospective forecasts freeze before kickoff. Research reconstructions are labeled and kept separate.
- Playoff fields use stored season rules and actual champions when present, otherwise provisional highest-ranked eligible conference teams.
- Ballot moves use insertion ordering. A submitted ballot locks and reveals team identity; drafts can be restarted rather than revised.

## Data-source and retention contract

**Current source:** CFBD validation failures include the request path and season/week parameters, one-based response record number, available football identifiers (such as player name, team, ID, and overall draft pick), and the failing field's expected type and bounded received value. Missing, null, and empty strings are distinguished. These messages flow into sync health and the owner action queue; HTTP failures include status and attempt count. Diagnostics omit whole payloads and redact the configured API key. Draft picks map CFBD's `name` into the application's `playerName`. Previously stored failures keep their old message until another sync updates the state; these improvements require a backend deployment to appear in hosted syncs.

Direct CFBD requests supply national programs, games, features, recruiting/talent/returning context, draft selections, and polls. Direct nflverse release files supply confirmed Michigan alumni only. Source failures retain good data and report staleness. Detailed national team-game features are pruned outside the rolling five-year window; compact games and frozen outputs remain.

## Operational commands

**Current source:** Michigan hockey's public roster page also provides a season-specific Schedule & results view, opponent filtering, and source links. `hockeySchedules.list/importSeason` owns isolated game history with owner authorization, per-season revisions, and idempotent official-ID updates. Internal `hockeyResults` automation adds Eastern-time-gated score refreshes, validated hockey player-game imports, pending retries, and correction receipts. Development validated the backend contract on 2026-10-24. See [hockey schedules](michigan-hockey-schedules.md) for the game-state, overnight processing, and review contract.

Michigan basketball has the same public schedule/history view through `basketballSchedules.list/importSeason`, with separate tables and basketball-specific score/tie validation. See [basketball schedules](michigan-basketball-schedules.md). Both sports share presentation and operator import logic while preserving independent data and revisions.

- `npm run migration:plan -- <legacy-export.json>` creates a no-write audit.
- `npm run migration:prepare -- <legacy-export.json> <new-directory> [season]` creates non-overwriting target JSONL plus an audit report.
- `npm run restore:prepare -- <backup.json> <new-directory>` verifies a version-2 or revision-bound version-3 backup fingerprint and prepares ordered JSONL without writing Convex.
- `npm run check` runs offline tests, type/lint, documentation links, and the production build.

The exact hosted cutover sequence lives in [Deployment](../guides/deployment.md).

Specialist tables (K/P/LS) retain a single Snaps column for special-teams totals. The separate ST Snaps column on offensive/defensive tables uses its own saved cutoff; missing totals remain unknown.

The Matrix Recruit column shows the saved 247Sports Composite overall rank and rating (for example, `#38 (0.9745)` or `#1 (1.000)`). It prefers the latest recruiting evaluation, falling back to the latest transfer evaluation with the source kind identified in the tooltip. Missing rank/rating components remain unknown; 0–100 provider scores are never presented as composite ratings.

The admin Roster spreadsheet has a Recruiting column group with editable 247Sports Composite overall rank and rating (0�1) for enrolled players and commitments. It loads the same preferred recruiting/transfer evaluation used by the public Recruit column. Saving an edited component preserves the other saved component and appends an owner-verified correction of the same evaluation kind, retaining history. Blank clears that component. Validation, owner authorization, revision checks, and batch audit receipts apply to these edits. New evaluation fields are optional in the mutation payload for older clients.

**Current source:** Michigan basketball and hockey default to [season home pages](michigan-season-home.md): sourced historical coaches, conference finishes and championships, player contributors and leaders, postseason results, and full schedules. Direct roster/stat and schedule views remain URL-addressable. Future tournament fixtures never establish qualification.

**Hockey NPI:** `hockeyNpi.forSchedule` exposes the selected season's current or final national NPI table and refresh availability. Internal `hockeyNpi.refresh` runs Mondays at 13:07 UTC, replaces current rankings, and retains one final table per completed season. See [hockey NPI](michigan-hockey-npi.md).

**Hockey talent indicator:** `hockeyDrafts.forSeason` provides sourced NHL career selections for players on the selected saved roster. Hockey uses NHL draft pick/year/round instead of recruiting fields; basketball retains recruiting. See [hockey draft history](michigan-hockey-drafts.md).

**Upcoming hockey draft:** `hockeyDrafts.forSeason` includes a nullable dated Elite Prospects projection for each matching player in the season preceding that draft. Internal compare-and-set imports preserve completed selections and annual roster values. See [hockey draft history](michigan-hockey-drafts.md).
