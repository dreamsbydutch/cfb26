# Rating improvement research sources — 2026-09-12

**Historical research note:** this records the hypotheses before implementation, combining code findings, preliminary diagnostics, and primary-source research. Subsequent validation and current behavior belong to the [three-rating contract](three-team-ratings.md).

## Application audit and recommended order

**Historical research baseline:** Power v3, Program v2, and Resume v3 at commit `3ae8a07`. The research itself changed no live model. The subsequent implementation and evaluated decisions are recorded in [Three team ratings](three-team-ratings.md) and the [improvement evaluation](power-improvements-evaluation-2026-09-12.json). Recommendations below describe the starting hypotheses, not remaining release work.

1. **Fix Program acquisition normalization.** In [ratings.ts](../../../convex/ratings.ts), `profile.talent ?? profile.recruitingPoints` pools raw values from two different metrics before `evidencePercentiles`. A fallback therefore changes units as well as source. Compute within-season percentiles separately on comparable cohorts, then select a normalized fallback; audit overlap calibration and source coverage. Add missing-talent and equal-relative-standing fixtures. This is a correctness opportunity, not a demonstrated Power error reduction.
2. **Separate margin scaling from probability calibration.** Current Power shrinks/caps training margins but directly interprets rating differences as forecast points and applies a fixed logistic curve. In an exploratory rolling diagnostic on 5,969 games from 2018-2023 and 2025, fitting `actualMargin = scale * predictedMargin + homeOffset * nonNeutral` by least squares using only preceding evaluation seasons reduced MAE from 13.447144 to 13.152030. Keeping the fixed probability curve worsened Brier from .166044 to .169001 and calibration error from .018567 to .052693: do not ship this alone. Separately, fitting the existing logistic calibrator on earlier seasons improved Brier to .165776 and calibration error to .011289, leaving margin MAE unchanged. Joint calibration is the next experiment, not an already validated combination. These seven folds reuse model-selection data and do not satisfy the eight-season promotion requirement. [Diagnostic artifact](rating-opportunity-diagnostics-2026-09-12.json)
3. **Test a stable home-field model.** [ratingSystem.ts](../../../convex/ratingSystem.ts) resets home advantage to 2.5 each season and jointly refits team effects from that season's home results with ten pseudo-observations. On the inspected Week 2 edition, Rutgers' estimate was .08 after its home defeat; this is a warning about confounding venue with team strength, not proof that its true advantage is .08. Compare a fixed common effect, a historically learned shared effect, and heavily pooled venue effects fitted only before the forecast. Preserve neutral-field symmetry and keep home advantage outside the rank.
4. **Add independent efficiency evidence.** Test the per-game, opponent-adjusted efficiency blend below. This is the largest feature addition worth prioritizing, but its gain is unmeasured. Audit actual access and coverage first; existing season-stat client support does not mean the active Power publication consumes per-game efficiency.
5. **Improve offseason and FCS evidence.** Use verified continuity to vary confidence, and obtain FCS-versus-FCS results. Do not apply brand or conference bonuses, arbitrary newcomer ceilings, or a blanket transfer penalty. Compare first-four-week and transition cohorts as well as the entire field.
6. **Improve Program's durable-health measurement.** [programRating.ts](../../../convex/programRating.ts) drops the age-four season weight from .40 to .15 at age five. Test a smooth decay curve matching the agreed recent-five/older-five emphasis. Draft output currently measures absolute NFL production, not development relative to incoming talent. Test a bounded talent-to-draft conversion signal alongside absolute output, with recruiting-cohort alignment and transfer attribution; using same-year recruiting as draft input would be invalid. These are product-policy candidates, not automatic fixes.
7. **Improve Resume's performance credit.** Keep actual wins and losses authoritative, with the approved 70% results / 30% performance split. Test competitive-drive dominance and possession normalization within the performance portion, so late cosmetic touchdowns do not equal sustained control. Opponent quality must remain current-season-only; avoid pregame Power-based win-probability feeds in this calculation.
8. **Evaluate record difficulty as a Resume challenger.** Current results credit is wins minus expected wins. A contender with game win probabilities [.9,.1] and one with [.5,.5] both expect one win, so going 2-0 earns the same results credit; their chances of 2-0 are .09 and .25. A schedule's full record distribution contains information that the sum loses. Compare a strength-of-record tail probability as a separate diagnostic first. Any replacement changes the merit policy and must preserve win/loss monotonicity and avoid giving positive credit for a loss. Committee agreement is not ground truth.

## Repair the evaluation boundary first

[scripts/evaluate-power.mjs](../../../scripts/evaluate-power.mjs) excludes an entire year unless every retained schedule entry is completed. The retained 2024 export has exactly one unfinished entry: App State-Liberty, Week 5. App State confirms the game was canceled by Hurricane Helene. [Official schedule](https://appstatesports.com/sports/football/schedule/2024)

Represent confirmed cancellations separately from pending or missing results, then include the completed 2024 games in evaluation. Do not merely accept every unfinished game as canceled. This recovers a modern evaluation season without inventing a result. Check selection/final publication readiness for the same issue.

The current v3 diagnostic has MAE 14.523479 in Weeks 1-4 and 12.831798 in Weeks 9-12; those cohorts have different opponents and game types, so this is a prioritization signal rather than a controlled causal comparison. Postseason has only 313 games and calibration error .108736; audit neutral designation, availability and opt-outs before adding a postseason adjustment. Preserve source cutoffs, test missing-data fallbacks on the full field, use nested tuning, and start a frozen prospective challenger stream for upcoming games.

## Coverage and historical validity

CFBD documents drives and raw plays from 2001, box scores from 2004, returning production from 2014, team talent from 2015, and portal data from 2021. These ranges do not guarantee complete teams, games, subdivisions, or fields. Returning production is explicitly a preseason dataset for the named season. Historical records can be corrected after publication. [Data availability](https://api.collegefootballdata.com/data-availability)

For each proposed feature, persist the source retrieval time, football season, game ID where applicable, publication boundary when available, and coverage status. A modern reconstruction of an old preseason roster is not proof of what was known at that time. Likewise, importing final-season opponent-adjusted ratings into an earlier week's forecast would leak later results. Use archived snapshots or clearly label reconstruction limitations.

Audit missingness by season, conference, subdivision, and team before fitting. Compare new and old models on the same covered games, and also on the entire field using the specified fallback. Otherwise a model can appear better merely because it skips the hardest or least-covered games.

## Access and cost

The current CFBD tier page advertises **1,000 calls per month** for Free, including historical data, team/player statistics, recruiting, and advanced metrics. It lists opponent-adjusted metrics starting at Tier 1, live play-by-play at Tier 2, and GraphQL at Tier 3. [API access tiers](https://collegefootballdata.com/api-tiers)

This is a published entitlement description, not a successful probe of this application's key. Verify access and remaining quota before a backfill. Prefer weekly or season-level aggregate requests, cache completed seasons, and reserve quota for existing operations. A route appearing in the API reference does not establish that this key can use it. The candidates above do not require buying a feed or importing another provider's final ratings.

## What would establish improvement

For Power, predeclare features and compare rolling forecasts against the current incumbent using margin MAE, Brier score, calibration, and season/cohort splits. Parameter fitting and probability calibration must use earlier data than the test fold. Retain a final untouched season or begin a prospective challenger stream; repeatedly selecting on the same eight seasons weakens evidence.

For Program, evaluate durable multi-year outcomes and sensitivity to missing acquisition/development data; tomorrow's score is not its objective. For Résumé, test that better wins and better performances receive appropriate credit without historical talent leakage, along with schedule and coverage sensitivity. Matching a committee poll is a comparison, not an objective ground truth for deservingness.
