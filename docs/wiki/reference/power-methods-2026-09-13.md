# Published Power methods and reproducible adaptations

[Reference index](README.md) · [Three team ratings](three-team-ratings.md) · [Power foundation evaluation](power-foundation-2026-09-13.md)

Research checked September 13, 2026. **Current published methods** below means documented publisher behavior, with the source's date retained; it does not certify a complete 2026 algorithm or a current ranking edition. **Recommended implementation** means an experimental design, not shipped behavior or demonstrated accuracy.

**Approved direction:** SP+ and FPI are benchmarks first. They may be tested as ensemble inputs only with verified publisher editions and dated pregame availability; they must demonstrate predictive improvement before admission. No external rating currently enters the three published models.

## Benchmark editions and historical availability

**Current API contract:** CFBD's SP+ endpoint accepts season/team; FPI accepts season/team/conference. Their documented responses lack publisher release time, through-week and historical edition identifiers. Elo accepts week and season type, defaulting to the latest available week when omitted. A weekly parameter still requires verification of whether the selected values precede or follow that week's games. [CFBD ratings API](https://api.collegefootballdata.com/api/ratings)

**Recommended implementation:** store `retrievedAt`, source URL, payload hash, provider, publisher, season, publisher edition, publication time, and through-week separately. Unknown publisher fields remain unknown. Downloading a value today proves observation today, not that it is today's publisher edition. A discrepancy such as the reported Clemson 50-versus-40 mismatch requires edition reconciliation; neither repeated downloading nor a local forecast timestamp resolves it. This note does not independently verify either rank.

Use explicit statuses: **publisher edition verified**, **provider snapshot; publisher edition unknown**, and **superseded/mismatched**. Archive newly observed publisher editions prospectively. Historical season-level SP+/FPI values cannot serve as earlier weekly predictions without additional dated evidence. A neutral rating difference plus an assumed home adjustment is a derived benchmark, not a publisher's matchup forecast.

Retrospective revisions matter even when the rating formula is documented: KenPom's 2016 announcement says past ratings were recomputed, while past predictions were not. A historical ratings page and an archived pregame forecast therefore need different provenance. [KenPom methodology update](https://kenpom.com/blog/ratings-methodology-update/)

## Recommended implementation: independent football challengers

1. **Adjusted efficiency:** fit separate offensive and defensive effects around a national baseline using only prior games. Start with per-game PPA, success rate and their sample counts; compare per-play and per-drive variants. CFBD supplies game-level advanced statistics and a garbage-time exclusion option. Record that option and source coverage with every export. [CFBD statistics API](https://api.collegefootballdata.com/api/stats)
2. **Football context:** explicitly handle field position, scoring opportunities, defensive/special-teams scores, overtime, kneeldowns and short possessions before adopting basketball-style possession normalization. Estimate football pace and home advantage from earlier seasons. Keep sparse teams and FCS opponents in the adjustment graph with shrinkage and visible missingness. Compare competitive-play filtering against unfiltered data on identical games; do not assume filtering helps.
3. **Sequential Elo:** implement an independent result-based probability model from completed games. Tune learning rate, offseason regression and home adjustment chronologically. Test a margin variant separately, including favorite correction. Do not label arbitrary rescaling of the existing Power rating as an independent Elo model. Learn any Elo-to-point-margin mapping from earlier predictions and results.
4. **Roster priors:** add verified preseason talent and continuity only through dated, coverage-aware inputs. Track quarterback evidence separately when available. Current downloads of historical roster data are reconstructions unless archived availability is proved.
5. **Ensemble:** combine independently generated margin, efficiency and Elo forecasts through constrained weights learned from earlier out-of-fold predictions. Calibrate probabilities independently of the point scale. Retain a simpler challenger if additional components have correlated errors and do not improve held-out forecasts. External proprietary ratings remain benchmarks rather than required model inputs.

## Current research implementation

The [Elo screening artifact](power-elo-evaluation-2026-09-13.json) evaluates a separately implemented, results-only Elo model and fixed 10%, 20%, and 30% blends with Power v5. No external rating enters this experiment. The research policy uses K=24, offseason retention=.8 toward observed subdivision means, and a 55-Elo home effect. These are explicit candidate settings, not established college-football optima. Elo-to-point and probability calibration use earlier seasons only; evaluation uses the incumbent's exact weekly cutoffs and games. Future games and their outcomes cannot enter earlier forecasts.

| Results-only Elo   |            15.3406 |              18.2427 | .185303 |

All Elo challengers failed promotion. This rejects these settings and blends, not every Elo formulation. Reproduce the screening with `node scripts/evaluate-power-components.mjs <enriched-data.json> <incumbent-evaluation.json> <market-directory> <new-output.json>`.

The [independent efficiency experiment](power-efficiency-evaluation-2026-09-13.json) fits additive offensive and defensive PPA effects around the observed national baseline. It uses a three-season observation window, half weight per older season, 150-play ridge shrinkage per unit, at most 80 weighted plays per game, 35 fitting iterations, and an explicit 2.5-point venue assumption. The 65-points-per-net-PPA conversion and probabilities are calibrated with earlier forecasts. Missing effects fall back to neutral values and remain in the scored sample. Historical advanced-stat availability is reconstructed; this is research code, not a live ingestion path or a claim to reproduce proprietary SP+ or KenPom.

The 10% efficiency blend reduced overall margin MAE to **12.8385** and early-season MAE to **13.4003**, but Brier worsened to **.163738** and the joint season-fold gate failed. Larger blends and the standalone model also failed. Neither new component is promoted. Append `efficiency` to the evaluator command to reproduce this historical fixed-weight experiment. The learned ensemble below supersedes fixed percentages as the current ensemble research approach; tuning the component models' internal parameters remains recommended work.

### Learned component weights and combined probabilities

**Current research; not promoted:** [The learned ensemble evaluation](power-learned-ensemble-2026-09-13.json) combines Power v5, independent results-only Elo, and opponent-adjusted PPA. It learns nonnegative weights summing to one by minimizing squared margin error on earlier out-of-season forecasts. Every component may receive zero weight. An exact active-face solver evaluates the constrained least-squares solution, including boundary solutions; there is no selected percentage grid. Squared error is the declared fitting objective; promotion still requires the existing MAE, Brier, calibration, season-regression and early-season benchmark gates.

The chronology is nested. Replayed 2015 forecasts seed component scale calibration; 2016 starts the component training cohort; 2017 supplies the first ensemble predictions whose weights were fitted to an earlier season. For each outer test year from 2018 through 2025, weights use only preceding seasons. A separate symmetric logistic fit uses only earlier ensemble predictions whose own weight fitting excluded their season. This prevents probability calibration from reusing margins fitted to those same outcomes. Probabilities come from the combined margin, not an average of component probabilities. The logistic fit retains the shared calibrator's weak slope regularization and 1%–99% bounds; neutral label reversal produces complementary probabilities. Power v5's original outer-fold forecasts remain exactly unchanged in the comparison.

| ---------------- | -----------------: | -------------------: | ------------: | --------------: |
| Power v5         |          12.844823 |            13.433302 |       .163680 |         .125794 |
| Learned ensemble |          12.842129 |            13.422009 |       .163771 |         .125776 |

The 2026 research fit learned **93.003% Power, 0% Elo, and 6.997% efficiency** from 8,635 earlier-season forecasts. Its probability slope is **0.1149665842**, fitted to 7,762 prior cross-fitted ensemble forecasts with zero intercept. These are experimental fitted coefficients, not live weights. Across the eight evaluated seasons, fitted Power weights ranged from 91.4% to 94.0%; Elo ranged from zero to 4.7%. Both models were compared on the same 1,392 early games with available opening lines: ensemble MAE was 13.4693, Power v5 13.4861, and market 12.3418. Those archived lines lack quote timestamps and are a limited benchmark.

**Decision:** retain Power v5. The ensemble slightly improved aggregate margin error and early Brier, but worsened aggregate Brier, materially regressed in 2020, and did not jointly improve margin and Brier in most seasons. No gate was relaxed. These reused development seasons and reconstructed historical inputs do not establish performance on an untouched holdout or superiority to SP+/FPI. External ratings remain excluded. Component internal settings are still the explicit research policies above; this experiment learns ensemble weights and probability calibration, not every parameter in those underlying models.

Reproduce with `node scripts/evaluate-power-ensemble.mjs <enriched-data.json> <incumbent-evaluation.json> <market-directory> <new-output.json>`. The output retains source/incumbent hashes, component calibration fits, annual learned weights, probability fits, future research coefficients, forecast hash, and promotion decisions. Cohort mismatches, duplicate forecasts and non-pregame cutoffs fail rather than silently dropping games. Regression tests change held-out outcomes and verify that the corresponding weights, probabilities and predicted margins cannot change.

Benchmark ingestion now also captures FPI, with both SP+ and FPI marked publisher-edition unverified by default. `scripts/lib/benchmark-vintage.mjs` requires verified publisher season, completed week, publication/verification times, source URL, a publisher-content hash, and a matching downloaded-rows hash before a copy qualifies. Verification means reconciling the actual rows against the publisher; inserting metadata alone does not establish that factual check. The freeze and scoring commands reject legacy unverified archives. The old September 13 SP+ artifact is preserved for audit and is excluded from current benchmark scoring.

## Recommended evaluation contract

Freeze each slate's inputs before kickoff. Within each outer season, tune features, priors, shrinkage, blowout treatment and ensemble weights using earlier seasons only. Earlier-season opponent adjustments must also exclude later games. Once development choices have been inspected against a season, that season is development evidence; repeated selection does not create an untouched holdout.

Score margin MAE and RMSE, probability Brier and log loss, and calibration on identical games. Report early season, late season, postseason, large favorites, conferences, FCS opponents and missing-data cohorts. Report paired uncertainty with season/week grouping rather than treating correlated forecasts as independent. Also score full-field fallbacks so coverage selection cannot manufacture improvement.

Compare against the incumbent, simple margin and Elo baselines, and edition-verified external forecasts. Timestamped market quotes require a common forecast horizon; a retrospectively archived line without quote time is a limited benchmark. Promote only after prespecified improvement and calibration gates plus a frozen prospective trial. No source above establishes that this proposed ensemble will beat SP+, FPI or the market.
