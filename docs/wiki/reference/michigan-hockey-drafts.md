# Michigan hockey NHL draft history

[Reference index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

**Current source:** hockey uses NHL draft position as its talent indicator. The public Roster & stats tables replace Recruit with NHL draft. Each compact cell shows the drafting team's logo alongside overall pick, draft year, and round; expanding it shows the full team name. The pre-Michigan selection flag is not displayed. Source records remain internal. The displayed selection is a career fact and may postdate the selected roster season. Basketball retains recruiting rank/rating. Hockey's owner spreadsheet omits recruiting fields; older saved null recruiting keys remain accepted when editing a roster, but new hockey recruiting values are rejected.

**Team logos:** `src/features/teams/nhlTeamLogos.json` retains official NHL SVG asset URLs reviewed against the [NHL standings API](https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/now) and historical club schedules, using the [NHL endpoint reference](https://github.com/Zmalski/NHL-API-Reference). Historical drafting names retain their own logo, including Phoenix, Atlanta, Arizona, the Mighty Ducks, and Utah Hockey Club. Team-name matching ignores accents and punctuation. Browsers load the images directly from NHL's asset host; this display adds no Python package, backend request, or per-player API call. Unknown teams and failed images fall back to the team name. The existing pre-Michigan field remains internal source data.

**Hosted coverage:** the requested [Michigan Athletics NHL draft history](https://mgoblue.com/news/2008/6/27/michigan_players_selected_in_the_nhl_draft) is updated through 2026 despite its 2008 URL. Its entry/amateur table matches 82 distinct players in the saved 2005–06 through 2026–27 rosters after reviewed name aliases. Official [NHL draft records](https://records.nhl.com/site/api/draft) add 15 players omitted from that list, including transfers and incoming players. Production contains 97 players' draft selections, matching 261 annual roster entries. Unmatched players show an unknown dash; absence from these sources does not establish undrafted status.

NHL records resolve two disagreements with the Michigan article: Lee Moffie was selected in round 7, pick 188 in 2010 (Michigan lists round 6); Aidan Park was selected at pick 223 in round 7 in 2025 (Michigan lists 233). The NHL records, not the conflicting article values, are retained for these two players. The drafting franchise is a historical fact, not the player's current rights holder. Michigan's explicit pre-attendance marker is retained when the article is the selected source; that status remains unknown for NHL-only records.

## NHL career statistics

**Current source:** selecting a player's name in the hockey Roster & stats view opens a keyboard-accessible **NHL career** dialog with career totals and season-by-season club rows, separate Regular season and Playoffs views, and goalie-specific GP/W/L/OTL/SV%/GAA/SO columns. Skaters show GP/G/A/PTS/+/−/PIM. The history covers the entire NHL career, including years after the selected Michigan season. Traded seasons retain each club's row; redundant combined-team rows are omitted. Official career totals come from the NHL rather than summing club rows. Missing values remain dashes.

The public `hockeyNhl.forPlayer` action accepts only a saved `teamRosterEntries` ID. Its internal identity query rejects other sports and uses the indexed draft history, capped at five selections. A bounded NHL player search finds exact normalized full-name candidates, including the existing reviewed nickname aliases. At most three candidate landing pages are fetched. Identity requires either Michigan college attendance in the selected season or a corroborating saved draft year/round/pick within that player's college era. College matching recognizes NCAA, CCHA, and Big Ten labels and explicit Michigan university-name variants; Michigan State and ambiguous identities do not match. This also supports undrafted alumni when college history corroborates them. Unverified identities display unknown NHL appearances; a verified profile with no NHL appearances displays that distinct empty state. Network failures display a retry control.

The integration calls the NHL's public [player search](https://search.d3.nhle.com/api/v1/search/player?culture=en-us&limit=100&q=Hughes) and [player landing endpoint](https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/player/8480800/landing) directly, as described in the supplied [API reference](https://github.com/Zmalski/NHL-API-Reference). Each HTTP request has a ten-second timeout. History loads only when opened and is cached by roster ID in React Query for fifteen minutes. The dialog includes an NHL player-profile link; retrieval timestamps remain internal. There is no Python package, new database table, automatic import, or change to saved college statistics. This source change does not establish that the backend or web feature has been deployed.

## Storage and matching

**Upcoming-draft rankings:** the NHL draft cell also supports dated [Elite Prospects consolidated rankings](https://www.eliteprospects.com/draft-center/consolidated-ranking). Projections display only `Proj. #rank` and draft year, without an extra disclosure. Source/profile links and collection dates remain in the stored record. These are ranking observations, never completed draft picks or recruiting ratings. Only a matched saved player on the roster immediately preceding that draft year receives a projection. Missing players stay unknown. Completed selections take display precedence automatically.

`hockeyDraftProjections` retains one current observation per player key/draft year with rank, source/profile URLs, collection time, and a hash of the reviewed observation JSON. Its player/year index supports the existing public season query. Internal `hockeyDrafts.importProjection` validates exact hockey roster matches, source/profile identity, rank bounds, observation date, and expected previous observation before replacing a rank; identical replays are no-ops, stale observations are rejected, and already-drafted players cannot receive a projection. It does not update roster fields or completed selections. There is no automatic Elite Prospects refresh: direct server fetches returned HTTP 403, while the research browser exposed the public ranking. Updates require another reviewed source observation; collection dates stay available internally.

`hockeyDraftSelections` stores source-backed selections with player name/key, year, round, overall pick, drafting team, nullable pre-Michigan indicator, source URL/hash, and import time. The source hash covers Michigan's raw HTML or the canonical JSON NHL record, respectively. Indexes cover player key/year and year/pick. Draft data is independent of annual roster values, statistics, roster revisions, schedule imports, NPI, and football data. Full Convex exports cover it.

The public `hockeyDrafts.forSeason` query starts with the selected hockey roster (at most 101 entries), then uses indexed per-player reads capped at five career selections. There is no public draft write. Internal `hockeyDrafts.importMatched` validates the source, pick, and matching saved hockey roster IDs; rejects cross-sport/unrelated identities and conflicting picks; and upserts idempotently. Imports neither create players nor rewrite their season values. Draft matching uses normalized full names and explicit nickname aliases, never surname-only or fuzzy joins. Reviewed preparation also checks draft year against the player's saved roster era: the 2025 Cameron Reid must not match the different 2009 draftee of the same name.

## Reproduction and import

The reviewed source directory outside Git contains `source.html`, complete `nhl-draft-records.json` (2000–2026), `nhl-teams.json`, and `rosters-before.json`. Raw public source evidence and review receipts are retained at `C:/Users/choug/.t3/artifacts/michigan-nhl-draft-20261019`. The preparation helper validates article structure and source completeness, keeps source hashes, identifies NHL-only matches, and fails on unreviewed source disagreements. Supplemental drafts are separate events outside the saved roster era and are not imported as entry-draft selections.

```powershell
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/prepare-hockey-drafts.mjs <source-directory> <new-reviewed.json>
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/import-hockey-drafts.mjs <reviewed.json> <deployment-name>
# After target authorization and a fresh full production export:
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/import-hockey-drafts.mjs <reviewed.json> <deployment-name> --apply
```

Preparation never overwrites an existing reviewed output. Import defaults to dry run, resolves roster IDs on the actual target, validates every reviewed selection before writing, and imports at most ten selections per transaction. It verifies every field through the public season query, asserts unchanged roster fields/revisions, and writes a receipt beside the reviewed file. A stopped import can be rerun safely. Development may contain fewer historical rosters than production; rehearsal uses only selections matching existing development players.
